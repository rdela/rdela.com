Ricky de Laveaga was born an artist and an [activist](https://artact.io/),
started making movies in high school, and never looked back. In addition to
producing and editing cinematic projects for your new favorite tiny movie
studio, [Famebot](https://famebot.com/), he designs and develops software and
websites, contributes to the [Lume](/archive/lume/) ecosystem, and writes prose
and&nbsp;poetry.
