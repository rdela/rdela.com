Ricky de Laveaga was born an artist, started making movies in high school, and
never looked back. In addition to producing and editing cinematic projects under
the banner of his up-and-coming studio, [Famebot](https://famebot.com/), he
designs and develops software, mostly for the web, and writes prose and poetry.
