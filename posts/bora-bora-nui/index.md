Turquoise water leads up to the beach and a lush green hill above the overwater
bungalows at the [Bora&nbsp;Bora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bora_Bora)
[Nui&nbsp;Resort](https://conradhotels3.hilton.com/en/hotels/french-polynesia/conrad-bora-bora-nui-PPTBNCI/index.html)
in [French&nbsp;Polynesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Polynesia), with
puffy white clouds in the sky over
[Motu&nbsp;Toopua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toopua).
[Mount&nbsp;Otemanu](https://tahititourisme.com/en-us/island/bora-bora/) can be
seen in the distance rising over the bluffs on the&nbsp;left.
