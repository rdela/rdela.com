Since I wrote it, I have been thinking of how to follow up my (unfortunately)
eerily prescient 2023 post,
[The Defenestration of Twitter](https://rdela.com/defenestration-twitter/). In
the post, I referenced [project REDMAP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/REDMAP)
and how its success indicated “how woefully unprepared we are to deal with
foreign influence operations” in the United States of America.

As we sit here three years later, my predictions, dire as they were, were not
sufficiently pessimistic. Greedy cowards funded by murderous tyrants denigrate
and destroy the livelihoods of Americans and foreigners alike. Dictators openly
suck the wealth and life blood from their people and hide it in
[nefarious banks](https://www.wnycstudios.org/podcasts/trumpinc/episodes/trump-inc-trump-deutsche-bank-its-complicated),
[phony currencies](https://www.citizen.org/article/donald-trump-crypto-investors-4-7-billion-underwater/),
[pyramid schemes](https://www.citationneeded.news/mapping-trumps-crypto-empire/),
[pump and dump scams](https://oversightdemocrats.house.gov/trump-family-corruption-tracker),
and
[money laundering](https://www.mcclatchydc.com/news/politics-government/white-house/article210477439.html)
real estate
[transactions](https://www.newsweek.com/manafort-trump-putin-russia-mafia-strzok-fbi-1076582)—amid
widespread confusion about
[who precisely is in charge](https://money.com/ivanka-trump-brand-company-retail-sales-china/)
of
[which country](https://kotaku.com/jared-kushner-reportedly-likes-playing-video-games-with-the-saudi-crown-prince-2000735875).

Gilbert Achcar titled his new book
[_Gaza Catastrophe: The Genocide in World-Historical Perspective_](www.ucpress.edu/books/the-gaza-catastrophe/).
I recommend listening to the entire interview with
[Achcar on the 30 April 2026 episode of Democracy Now](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/4/30/iran_war_update),
but here is a key segment (emphasis mine):

> It is absolutely obvious to me that _**for Donald Trump, for Kushner, Jared

> whatever Israel can represent. I mean, that’s where their economic interests
> are. That’s where they have billions of dollars of joint investments and the

Many honest, brave souls have fought and resisted this
[destruction](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/donaldtrump) of everything
that [keeps](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/robert-f-kennedy-jr) us
[safe](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/pete-hegseth), everything that we
hold [dear](https://www.theguardian.com/profile/rebeccasolnit). To those who
continue to summon the tenacity to persevere no matter the odds, I express
deepest sympathies and total solidarity.

To those who would harm not just their neighbors, but their own families,
children, grandchildren, and all generations to come, I can only express grave
concern for how your self-loathing and self-sabotage exponentially harm all of
us. With that said, it is important to convey that feeling pity or concern does
not condone judgement.

In judging the deluded as they fulfill their negative self-image, we delude
ourselves into the false notion that our own most mindless, irresponsible
moments would pass muster under such stringent criteria. Even when we think we
reserve our harshest judgements for ourselves, our aversion to the qualities we
most dislike in ourselves falls most heavily on others, since their qualifying
circumstances and mixed feelings are opaque to us. Moreover, even genocidal and
ecocidal forms of mass harm are not new phenomena, unnatural as they may be.

Fearful, violent, greedy demons within our own souls propel destruction of our
fragile home planet and allow desecrations to continue taking place. Yet
“demons” were once [daemons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daimon) who signify
“the experience of divine power” within us all. Ancient Himalayan teachings
[find expression](https://www.taramandala.org/programs-overview/long-term-study-pathways/feeding-your-demons/fyd-resources/feeding-your-demons-teachers/)
in [modern practices](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v0OI2mR9HzE&t=409s).

We are never too far from our past or our future to learn and arise together.

Let us become something more than what we have been.
